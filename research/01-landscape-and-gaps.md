# Research 1 — Sri Lanka tourism web landscape & gaps (via Perplexity, 2026-11-10)

Source thread: https://www.perplexity.ai/session/ee315cfa-8c07-4ca5-bbaf-f6c390f8fe01

## Existing sites
- **srilanka.travel** (Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau): official inspiration portal, attractions, events, weather notices. Broad, not decision-oriented; thin on transit, seasonality, niche interests.
- **sltda.gov.lk**: regulator; stats, licensing, policy (2025 Marine Tourism Roadmap). Not traveller-first. 2025 arrivals: 2,362,521 (+15.1%, record).
- **Sri Lanka Railways e-services**: timetable; needs offline verification.
- **Lonely Planet**: dependable editorial starting point; generic, slow to update.
- **Atlas Obscura**: ~46–52 unusual places (Biso Pokuna, Richmond Castle, Wewurukannala Vihara). Discovery-led, no logistics/timing/etiquette.
- **YAMU**: candid local voice, Colombo-centric.
- **Roar Media archive**: rich cultural features, but stopped publishing in 2024.
- **Reddit r/srilanka / TripAdvisor**: current & granular (monsoon trade-offs, scams, trains) but unstructured.

## Gaps identified
1. Transport that matches reality (disruptions, combos, fares)
2. **Two-coast monsoon planning** — scattered across forum comments; no clear seasonal decision tool coast-by-coast, incl. wildlife seasons and festivals, "if it rains do this instead"
3. Cultural context/etiquette explained (why, not just do/don't)
4. Heritage storytelling — "what to notice" not "what to see"
5. Lesser-known / slow travel
6. Accessibility
7. Honest budgeting
8. Wildlife calendar without greenwashing

Key line: the gap isn't missing content, it's a trusted synthesis layer that turns information into decisions.
