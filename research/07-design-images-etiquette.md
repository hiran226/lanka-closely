## Visual language (draw on, don't pastiche)
- Bawa / tropical modernism: deep eaves, verandahs, courtyards, calm grid, framed "courtyard" images, horizontal bands, whitespace.
- '43 Group (George Keyt): flat colour blocks, expressive line — do not imitate a specific artist.
- Kandyan painting (Degaldoruwa): terracotta/oxblood, ochre, black outlines, ivory, muted gold; narrative borders — use narrow rules sparingly.
- Ola palm-leaf manuscripts: narrow horizontal leaf format, warm fibre tones, binding holes, sequential reading.
- Lotus & liyavel scroll: restrained SVG ornament, secondary to photography.
- Laterite (rust brown), granite (grey), lime plaster (ivory) as surfaces.
- Direction: "field guide, not tourist brochure".
- Prefer original abstract ornament over scans of sacred paintings.

## Fonts (Google Fonts, OFL)
Noto Serif/Sans Sinhala, Noto Serif/Sans Tamil, Abhaya Libre (Sinhala), Mukta Malar (Tamil), Yaldevi (Sinhala). Latin: Fraunces, Newsreader, Source Serif 4. Give Sinhala/Tamil generous line-height.

## Images
- Wikimedia Commons best for specific monuments. Use CC0/PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA; avoid NC/ND. Credit: "Photo: Author, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0" + link to file page. Adapted (cropped) BY-SA images stay BY-SA.
- Unsplash/Pexels: free, no attribution required (appreciated); no standalone resale.
- CCF: ordinary photography included with tickets; professional/drone needs approval. Sigiriya fresco cave max 8 visitors at a time (SLTDA list); no flash/photos of frescoes enforced on site.

## Etiquette (sources: srilankatrip.com, Rough Guides, traveldojo, tikalanka, CCF)
- Remove shoes and hats — often for whole compound incl. stupa terraces.
- Cover shoulders & knees; white/light clothing traditional (esp. poya), not mandatory.
- Don't pose with your back to a Buddha image; don't climb plinths or use statues as props.
- Don't point soles of feet at Buddha images or monks.
- Walk clockwise around stupas & bodhi trees (keep it on your right).
- Moonstones: walk around rather than on them where possible (unsourced in answer — phrase gently; many are roped off).
- Ask before photographing people, rituals, monks; no flash where prohibited.
- Hindu kovils: some inner shrines closed to non-Hindus; men may be asked to remove shirts at some kovils (e.g. Nallur) — verify before stating.
- Buddha tattoos: cover them; travellers have been refused entry / deported (Immigrants & Emigrants Act 1948). Check current official advice.
