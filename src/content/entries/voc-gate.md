## What it meant

The Old Gate was completed by the Dutch in 1669, and its relief is a company logo in stone. A rooster stands on a rock above a shield carrying the VOC monogram of the Dutch East India Company, with a lion either side and the date ANNO MDCLXIX.

The British later added their own coat of arms on the outer face. The VOC emblem is on the inner face, so the gate shows one empire to arrivals and another to anyone leaving.

## How it changed

Galle's fortifications have Portuguese origins (Atlas Obscura gives 1588). The Dutch rebuilt them on a vast scale from the 1640s, and the British inherited and adapted them. UNESCO inscribed the Old Town of Galle and its Fortifications in 1988.

The layers are legible if you walk slowly. The bastions carry names such as Flag Rock, Triton, Aurora, Star, Sun and Moon. In the Groote Kerk, gravestones moved from the old Dutch cemetery pave the floor, carved with coats of arms, skulls and hourglasses. Verandah houses line the streets.

## Notice this

From outside you get the British arms. Walk through, turn round, and the Dutch rooster is waiting on its rock.
