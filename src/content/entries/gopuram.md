## What it meant

A gopuram is the monumental tiered gateway tower of a Dravidian temple. Its stacked registers are crowded with painted deities, and it marks the threshold between the street and the sacred precinct: a moonstone stood on end, if you like, announcing the crossing from a distance.

The colours are not decoration for its own sake. They identify the deities and narrate Puranic stories, so a tower can be read tier by tier.

## How it changed

Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil in Jaffna, dedicated to Murugan, shows how a temple renews itself. The present building, the fourth on the site, is usually dated to 1734, when Don Juan Ragunatha Maapaana Mudaliyar built it during the Dutch era; Tamil Wikipedia gives 1749. Its nine-storey Shanmuha Raja Gopuram, with the golden Swarna Vaasal entrance, was unveiled on 21 August 2011, and another raja gopuram followed in 2015.

During the Nallur festival, about 25 days in the Tamil month of Aavani (around August), the chariot (ther) procession brings the deity out through the streets.

## Notice this

Find Murugan among the crowd of figures by his vel and his peacock. Once you have him, the rest of the tower starts to sort itself out.
